STANDARD JOB TIME ESTIMATES (hours, end-to-end including setup)

--- OFFSET JOBS ---
1000 A4 flyers (1-colour):          2-3 hours total
  - Plate making: 30 min
  - Press setup: 30 min
  - Print run: 30-45 min
  - Drying: 30-60 min

5000 A4 brochures (4-colour, 2-sided):  6-8 hours total
  - Plate making (4 plates x 2 sides): 2 hours
  - Press setup + register: 90 min
  - Print run (2 passes): 2-3 hours
  - Drying between passes: 1-2 hours

Business cards (500 pieces, 4-colour):  3-4 hours
  - Plate + setup: 90 min
  - Print: 30 min
  - Lamination: 30 min
  - Die cutting: 30-60 min
  - Guillotine cutting: 30 min

--- DIGITAL JOBS ---
100 A4 copies (colour):             30-45 minutes
500 A4 copies (colour):             1-1.5 hours
1000 A4 copies (b&w):               1 hour
Variable data (500 pieces):         1.5-2 hours

--- FLEX / LARGE FORMAT ---
Single banner 4x6ft:                45-60 minutes
  - Design check + RIP: 15 min
  - Print: 20-30 min
  - Finishing (hem + eyelets): 15 min

10 banners 4x6ft:                   4-5 hours

--- FINISHING JOBS ---
Lamination (per 500 A4 sheets):     30-45 minutes
Die cutting (per 1000 sheets):      1-1.5 hours
Spiral binding (per 50 books):      1-1.5 hours
Perfect binding (per 50 books):     2-3 hours

--- SCHEDULING RULES OF THUMB ---
- Never schedule offset jobs back-to-back without 1 hour gap (drying + cleanup)
- Digital jobs can run back-to-back with 15 min gap
- Ink colours: each additional colour adds ~30 min setup
- Large format: allow 30 min per job for media loading
- Rush jobs add ~25% to total time (skipping queue, priority handling)
