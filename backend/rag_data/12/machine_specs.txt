COMMON PRINTING MACHINES — CAPABILITIES AND THROUGHPUT

--- OFFSET PRESS ---
Single-colour offset (e.g. Heidelberg GTO, Tata):
  - Speed: 5,000-8,000 impressions/hour
  - Max sheet size: typically 52x36cm or 72x52cm
  - Setup time per job: 30-45 minutes (plate change + makeready)
  - Typical job run: 1000-50,000 copies

Four-colour offset (e.g. Heidelberg SM52, KBA):
  - Speed: 8,000-15,000 sheets/hour
  - Setup time: 60-90 minutes (4 plates + colour registration)
  - Typical job run: 5,000+ copies (below this, digital is cheaper)

--- DIGITAL PRESS ---
Entry-level digital (e.g. Xerox B7030, Konica C3070):
  - Speed: 30-70 A4 pages/minute
  - Setup time: 5-10 minutes
  - Economic for short runs: 1-500 copies
  - Max resolution: 600-1200 DPI

High-volume digital (e.g. Xerox Iridesse, Konica AccurioPress):
  - Speed: 80-130 A4 pages/minute
  - Setup time: 10-15 minutes
  - Handles variable data printing

--- LARGE FORMAT / FLEX PRINTING ---
Solvent flex printer (e.g. Flora, Wit-Color):
  - Speed: 15-25 sqm/hour (standard quality)
  - Speed: 8-12 sqm/hour (high quality)
  - Media width: 1.6m to 3.2m
  - Setup time: 15-20 minutes per job

--- DIE CUTTING ---
Manual die cutting:
  - Speed: 800-1200 sheets/hour
  - Setup time: 30-45 minutes (die mounting)

Auto die cutting (e.g. Bobst, Heidelberg):
  - Speed: 3,000-7,000 sheets/hour
  - Setup time: 45-60 minutes

--- LAMINATION ---
Thermal lamination (pouch):
  - Speed: 5-8 sqm/minute
  - Types: gloss, matte, soft touch

Roll lamination:
  - Speed: 15-25 sqm/minute
  - Setup time: 10-15 minutes per roll change

--- MAINTENANCE INTERVALS ---
Offset press: full service every 500,000 impressions or 6 months
Digital press: drum replacement every 200,000 pages, developer every 100,000
Flex printer: head cleaning daily, head replacement every 12-18 months
