WELDING TIME STANDARDS (Indian MSME fabrication norms)

--- MIG WELDING (GMAW) ---
Butt weld, 5mm plate, per 100mm run:       4-6 minutes
Butt weld, 10mm plate, per 100mm run:      8-12 minutes (2 passes)
Fillet weld 6mm leg, per 100mm run:        3-5 minutes
Fillet weld 10mm leg, per 100mm run:       6-9 minutes

--- TIG WELDING (GTAW) ---
SS butt weld, 2mm, per 100mm run:          8-12 minutes
SS butt weld, 5mm, per 100mm run:          15-20 minutes
Aluminium butt weld, 3mm, per 100mm:       10-15 minutes
Root pass only (back purge): add 30% time

--- ARC WELDING (SMAW) ---
Butt weld, 6mm plate, per 100mm:           8-12 minutes
Structural fillet, per 100mm:              5-8 minutes
Overhead position: add 50% to flat position times

--- SETUP AND PREPARATION ---
Fit-up and tacking per joint:              5-20 minutes (depends on complexity)
Grinding/cleaning between passes:          3-5 minutes per 100mm
Post-weld grinding flush:                  4-8 minutes per 100mm
NDT (dye penetrant per joint):             10-15 minutes

--- TYPICAL FABRICATED STRUCTURES (total welding time) ---
Simple frame 1x1m (50x50 SHS):            3-5 hours
Platform 2x2m with handrail:              8-12 hours
Storage tank 1000 litres (MS):            12-18 hours
Conveyor frame 3m length:                  6-10 hours

--- PRODUCTIVITY FACTORS ---
Flat position (1G/1F):                    100% (reference)
Horizontal position (2G/2F):              80-85%
Vertical position (3G/3F):                70-75%
Overhead position (4G/4F):                55-65%

Experienced welder (5+ years):            100%
Semi-skilled welder (1-3 years):          70-80%
Trainee welder (<1 year):                 50-60%
