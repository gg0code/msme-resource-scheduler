FABRICATION WORK CENTERS — CAPABILITIES

--- CUTTING ---
Plasma cutter (handheld/CNC, e.g. Hypertherm, Ador):
  - Cuts: MS up to 50mm, SS up to 35mm, AL up to 25mm
  - Cut speed (10mm MS): 900-1200mm/min
  - Setup per job: 10-15 minutes

Angle grinder (cutting disc):
  - Max practical: 10mm plate, 75mm dia bar
  - Cut speed: slow, operator-dependent
  - Used for: site cuts, small quantities

Bandsaw (metal):
  - Cuts: solid bar up to 250mm dia
  - Cut speed: 15-30 min per cut (structural sections)
  - Setup: 5-10 minutes

--- BENDING ---
Press brake (e.g. Durma, Amada):
  - Capacity: typically 40-200 tonnes
  - Max length: 2m to 4m
  - Min bend radius: 1x material thickness
  - Setup time per job: 20-45 minutes
  - Bend cycle: 5-15 seconds per bend

Rolling machine (plate roll):
  - Capacity: 6-20mm plate, 2m width typical
  - Used for: cylinders, cones, curved plates
  - Setup: 30-45 minutes per job

--- DRILLING ---
Radial drill:
  - Capacity: up to 50mm dia in MS
  - Drilling speed (20mm hole in 10mm MS): 2-3 min per hole
  - Setup: 10-15 minutes

Magnetic drill:
  - Portable, used on large structures
  - Capacity: 12-50mm dia, depth up to 50mm
  - Setup: 5 minutes

--- SURFACE PREPARATION ---
Shot blast cabinet:
  - Throughput: 50-200 kg/hour
  - Finish: Sa 2.5 (near white metal)
  - Setup: 10 minutes

Angle grinder (flap disc):
  - Used for local surface prep, weld cleaning
  - Labour intensive: 0.1-0.3 sqm/hour

--- MAINTENANCE INTERVALS ---
Plasma cutter: nozzle/electrode replace every 50-200 starts
Press brake: hydraulic oil change every 2000 hours
Rolling machine: lubrication monthly, alignment check annually
