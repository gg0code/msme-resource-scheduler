STEEL GRADES FOR FABRICATION (Indian standards + common equivalents)

--- STRUCTURAL STEEL (IS 2062) ---
IS 2062 Grade A (E250):
  - Yield strength: 250 MPa minimum
  - Uses: general structural, columns, beams, frames
  - Weldability: excellent
  - Cost: standard reference (Rs 55-65/kg, 2025)

IS 2062 Grade B (E350):
  - Yield strength: 350 MPa minimum
  - Uses: heavy structures, high-load frames, cranes
  - Weldability: good (preheat required for thick sections >25mm)
  - Cost: ~10-15% premium over Grade A

--- PLATES AND SHEETS (IS 2062 / IS 3502) ---
MS (Mild Steel) Plate:
  - Grades: E250A, E250B, E250C
  - Available thickness: 5mm to 200mm
  - Uses: fabricated structures, tanks, housings
  - Cost: Rs 58-68/kg (2025)

HR Sheet (Hot Rolled, IS 10748):
  - Thickness: 1.6mm to 10mm
  - Uses: light fabrication, enclosures, platforms
  - Cost: Rs 60-70/kg

CR Sheet (Cold Rolled, IS 513):
  - Thickness: 0.5mm to 3.15mm
  - Better surface finish than HR
  - Uses: panels, doors, aesthetic components
  - Cost: Rs 72-85/kg

--- STAINLESS STEEL (IS 6911) ---
SS 304 (most common):
  - Uses: food processing equipment, tanks, pharma
  - Weldability: good (use 308L filler)
  - Cost: Rs 180-210/kg

SS 316 (marine/chemical grade):
  - Better corrosion resistance than 304
  - Uses: chemical equipment, coastal installations
  - Cost: Rs 230-270/kg

SS 202 (economy grade):
  - Uses: decorative, light duty
  - Not for structural or food contact
  - Cost: Rs 130-160/kg

--- SELECTION GUIDE ---
General structure, no corrosion risk:     IS 2062 Grade A (MS)
High load structure:                       IS 2062 Grade B
Food/pharma equipment:                    SS 304
Chemical equipment:                        SS 316
Budget decorative:                         SS 202
Outdoor structure (painted):              IS 2062 Grade A + epoxy primer

--- WASTAGE FACTORS ---
Plate cutting (plasma):   8-12% depending on part geometry
Plate cutting (laser):    5-8%
Tube/pipe cutting:        3-5%
Sheet metal (press brake): 5-10% for blanks
