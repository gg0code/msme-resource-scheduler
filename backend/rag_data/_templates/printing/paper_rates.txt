PAPER CONSUMPTION STANDARDS (Indian printing industry norms)

--- OFFSET PRINTING ---
A4 flyers (single side):
  - 1000 copies: ~1.1 reams (500 sheets per ream, 10% wastage included)
  - 5000 copies: ~5.5 reams
  - 10000 copies: ~11 reams

A4 brochures (4 pages, both sides):
  - 1000 copies: ~2.2 reams (folded, so 2 sheets per copy)
  - 5000 copies: ~11 reams

A3 posters (single side):
  - 1000 copies: ~2.2 reams (A3 = 2x A4, half the sheets fit per ream)
  - 5000 copies: ~11 reams

Business cards (standard 90x55mm, 8-up on A4):
  - 1000 cards: ~130 sheets
  - 5000 cards: ~630 sheets

Flex banners (per sqft, vinyl media not paper):
  - 1 sqft: ~1.1 sqft vinyl (10% wastage)
  - Standard banner 4x6ft: ~26.4 sqft vinyl

--- DIGITAL PRINTING ---
A4 digital (toner-based):
  - Wastage is lower: ~5% vs 10% for offset
  - 1000 copies: ~1.05 reams
  - 5000 copies: ~5.25 reams

--- PAPER WEIGHT GUIDE ---
70 GSM: standard office / economy flyers
80 GSM: good quality flyers, standard brochures
100 GSM: premium brochures, catalogues
130 GSM: high quality marketing material
170 GSM: thick brochures, folders
250 GSM+: business cards, covers

--- COST REFERENCE (approximate, INR, 2025) ---
70 GSM A4 (500 sheets): Rs 180-220
80 GSM A4 (500 sheets): Rs 220-270
100 GSM A4 (500 sheets): Rs 380-450
130 GSM A4 (500 sheets): Rs 520-600
250 GSM A4 (500 sheets): Rs 900-1100
Flex vinyl (per sqft): Rs 12-18
