SPARE PARTS MANAGEMENT — FIELD SERVICE NORMS

--- PARTS CLASSIFICATION ---
Class A (critical, high usage — always stock):
  - Consumables: filters, belts, seals, fuses, contactors
  - Rule: minimum 2-week stock at service depot
  - Review: weekly reorder check

Class B (moderate usage — stock 2-4 weeks):
  - Common wear parts: bearings, motors, pumps
  - Rule: 2-4 weeks stock
  - Review: monthly reorder check

Class C (low usage, high value — order on demand):
  - Major components: compressors, control panels, PCBs
  - Rule: 0-1 units in stock, supplier lead time tracked
  - Review: quarterly

--- COMMON CONSUMABLE PARTS (general reference) ---
Note: Replace this list with your actual product range.

Electrical consumables:
  - MCBs (miniature circuit breakers): keep common ratings (6A, 10A, 16A, 32A)
  - Contactors: keep top 3 rated sizes by installed base
  - Fuses: 10-pack minimum for each type used
  - Cable terminals: 100-pack per type

Mechanical consumables:
  - V-belts: keep top 5 cross-sections by installed base
  - Oil seals: keep top 10 sizes by installed base
  - Bearings: keep top 5 SKF/FAG sizes by installed base
  - O-rings: assortment kit minimum

Lubrication:
  - General purpose grease (lithium, NLGI 2): 5kg tin
  - Hydraulic oil (ISO 46): 20 litre drum
  - Gear oil (EP 90): 5 litre pack

--- PARTS INVENTORY RULES ---
Minimum stock value at depot: Rs 50,000-1,00,000 (depending on business size)
Maximum dead stock threshold: parts not used in 12 months — review for disposal
Parts ordering trigger: when stock falls below 2-week consumption rate
Emergency parts procurement: identify 3 alternate suppliers per critical part

--- VAN STOCK (what each technician carries) ---
Basic toolkit:
  - Hand tools: screwdrivers, spanners, pliers, multimeter
  - Safety: gloves, safety glasses, insulated tools
  - Consumables: common fuses, terminal blocks, small fasteners
  - Test equipment: multimeter, clamp meter

Additional for mechanical technician:
  - Common bearings and seals (top 5 sizes)
  - Lubricants: grease gun + 1 cartridge, small oil can
  - Gasket material: sheet gasket, RTV sealant

Additional for electrical technician:
  - MCBs (6A to 32A, 1-2 each)
  - Common contactors (2-3 sizes)
  - Cable: 5m each of 1.5sqmm and 2.5sqmm
  - Heat shrink, cable ties, insulation tape
