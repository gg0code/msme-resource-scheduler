SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT STANDARDS (Indian field service norms)

--- RESPONSE TIME BENCHMARKS ---
Critical (P1) — equipment down, business stopped:
  - Response: within 2-4 hours
  - Resolution target: same day or next business day
  - Escalation if breached: immediate manager alert

High (P2) — equipment degraded, partial operation:
  - Response: within 4-8 hours (same business day)
  - Resolution target: within 2 business days
  - Escalation if breached: after 8 hours

Medium (P3) — minor issue, workaround available:
  - Response: within 1 business day
  - Resolution target: within 5 business days

Low (P4) — cosmetic, no operational impact:
  - Response: within 2 business days
  - Resolution target: within 10 business days

--- VISIT DURATION BENCHMARKS ---
Preventive maintenance visit:       2-4 hours
Minor repair (part replacement):    1-2 hours
Major repair (component overhaul):  4-8 hours
New installation:                   4-8 hours (simple), 1-3 days (complex)
Commissioning + training:           1-2 days

--- FIRST-TIME FIX RATE TARGETS ---
Industry benchmark (good):          70-80% first-time fix
Industry benchmark (excellent):     85%+
Common causes of repeat visits:
  - Wrong part carried (most common)
  - Misdiagnosis of fault
  - Intermittent fault not reproducible on site

--- PARTS AVAILABILITY RULES ---
Fast-moving parts (consumables):    Keep 2-week stock at depot
Slow-moving parts (major components): Order on demand, 2-5 day lead time
Critical spares:                    Always 1 unit in stock per 20 installed base

--- TECHNICIAN ALLOCATION ---
Single technician:    Jobs up to 4 hours, equipment weight under 50kg
Two technicians:      Jobs over 4 hours, equipment over 50kg
Specialist + helper:  Complex overhauls, electrical + mechanical combined

--- SERVICE CALL COSTING (INR reference, 2025) ---
Labour rate (skilled technician): Rs 400-600/hour
Labour rate (helper): Rs 200-300/hour
Travel charge (per km): Rs 8-15 depending on vehicle
Minimum call charge: Rs 500-800 (covers first hour + travel within 10km)
Emergency/after-hours surcharge: 50-100% of normal rate
