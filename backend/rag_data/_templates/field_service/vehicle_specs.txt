FIELD SERVICE VEHICLES — CAPABILITIES AND COST NORMS

--- LIGHT COMMERCIAL VEHICLES (LCV) ---
Tata Ace / Mahindra Jeeto (mini truck):
  - Payload: 0.75-1.0 tonnes
  - Uses: parts delivery, small equipment transport
  - Running cost: Rs 8-12/km (fuel + maintenance)
  - Driver cost: Rs 15,000-20,000/month

Tata 407 / Eicher (medium truck):
  - Payload: 2-4 tonnes
  - Uses: heavy equipment, bulk parts
  - Running cost: Rs 15-22/km
  - Driver cost: Rs 18,000-25,000/month

--- TWO-WHEELERS (technician dispatch) ---
Motorcycle (100-125cc, e.g. Hero Splendor):
  - Fuel: 60-70 km/litre
  - Running cost: Rs 1.5-2.5/km
  - Uses: single technician + small tool kit

Scooter (e.g. Honda Activa):
  - Fuel: 45-55 km/litre
  - Storage: underseat compartment for small parts
  - Running cost: Rs 2-3/km

--- TRAVEL TIME BENCHMARKS (urban India) ---
Within city (0-15 km):          30-60 minutes
City outskirts (15-30 km):      45-90 minutes
Highway (30-80 km):             45-90 minutes
Rural roads (add 50% to highway time)

--- VEHICLE MAINTENANCE INTERVALS ---
Motorcycle/scooter:
  - Engine oil: every 3,000 km
  - Full service: every 6,000 km or 6 months
  - Tyre: every 25,000-35,000 km

LCV (Tata Ace class):
  - Engine oil: every 5,000 km
  - Full service: every 10,000 km or 6 months
  - Tyre: every 40,000-60,000 km

--- FUEL COST REFERENCE (INR, 2025) ---
Petrol: Rs 95-105/litre (varies by city)
Diesel: Rs 88-98/litre
CNG:    Rs 72-85/kg (where available)

--- VEHICLE ALLOCATION RULES ---
Heavy equipment service call:    LCV minimum
Multi-technician team (3+):      LCV or larger
Single technician, light tools:  Two-wheeler
Emergency/priority call:         Nearest available vehicle regardless of type
