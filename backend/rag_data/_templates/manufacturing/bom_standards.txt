BILL OF MATERIALS STANDARDS (Indian MSME manufacturing norms)

--- METAL FABRICATION COMPONENTS ---
Standard steel bracket (150x100x5mm, MS):
  - Raw MS plate required: ~0.12 kg per piece (includes 10% cutting wastage)
  - Machining time: 8-12 minutes per piece
  - Finish: powder coat adds 15-20 min per batch of 50

Mild steel shaft (25mm dia, 300mm length):
  - Raw bar stock required: ~1.4 kg per piece (25mm dia MS bar)
  - Turning time: 15-20 minutes per piece
  - Grinding (if required): 10-15 min per piece

--- ASSEMBLY OPERATIONS ---
Simple 3-component assembly:
  - Cycle time: 5-8 minutes per unit
  - Batch setup: 15-20 minutes
  - QC check: 2-3 minutes per unit (10% sampling)

Complex 10+ component assembly:
  - Cycle time: 20-35 minutes per unit
  - Batch setup: 30-45 minutes
  - QC check: 5-10 minutes per unit (full check)

--- WASTAGE FACTORS BY PROCESS ---
CNC machining:          8-12% material wastage
Manual turning:         10-15% material wastage
Sheet metal cutting:    10-20% wastage (depends on nesting efficiency)
Welding (filler wire):  15-20% overconsumption vs theoretical
Powder coating:         10% paint overconsumption

--- MATERIAL COST REFERENCE (INR, 2025) ---
Mild steel (MS) plate:    Rs 55-65/kg
Stainless steel (304):    Rs 180-220/kg
Aluminium (6061):         Rs 180-210/kg
Copper:                   Rs 750-850/kg
Standard fasteners (M8):  Rs 8-12 per piece

--- CAPACITY PLANNING RULES ---
- One CNC operator can run 1-2 machines simultaneously
- Manual lathe: 1 operator per machine always
- Assembly line: 1 supervisor per 5 assembly workers
- Quality inspection: 1 QC per 8 production workers
