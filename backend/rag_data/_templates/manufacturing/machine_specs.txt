MANUFACTURING WORK CENTERS — CAPABILITIES AND THROUGHPUT

--- CNC MACHINING CENTERS ---
VMC (Vertical Machining Center, e.g. BFW, Jyoti):
  - Spindle speed: 6,000-10,000 RPM
  - Table size: typically 800x400mm to 1000x500mm
  - Setup time per new job: 45-90 minutes
  - Tool change time: 3-5 seconds automatic
  - Typical batch: 10-500 pieces

HMC (Horizontal Machining Center):
  - Higher volume than VMC, 4-face machining possible
  - Setup time: 60-120 minutes
  - Suited for: housings, brackets, large prismatic parts

--- TURNING CENTERS ---
CNC Lathe (e.g. ACE, Bharat Fritz Werner):
  - Chuck size: 165mm to 210mm
  - Bar stock capacity: up to 65mm dia
  - Cycle time: 3-30 min per piece (depends on complexity)
  - Setup: 30-60 minutes per job

Manual Lathe:
  - Speed: depends on operator skill
  - Setup: 15-30 minutes
  - Suited for: prototypes, repair, low volume

--- GRINDING ---
Surface grinder:
  - Tolerance achievable: +/- 0.005mm
  - Feed rate: 0.5-2mm/pass
  - Setup: 20-30 minutes

Cylindrical grinder:
  - Tolerance: +/- 0.003mm
  - Setup: 30-45 minutes

--- WELDING STATIONS ---
MIG welding:
  - Deposition rate: 2-5 kg/hour
  - Setup per job: 15-20 minutes

TIG welding (precision):
  - Deposition rate: 0.5-2 kg/hour
  - Setup: 20-30 minutes
  - Used for: stainless, aluminium, thin sections

--- MAINTENANCE INTERVALS ---
CNC machining center: full PM every 500 hours runtime
Manual lathe: lubrication weekly, service every 6 months
Welding machine: contact tip replacement every 8-16 hours
Grinding wheel: dress after every 2-4 hours of cutting
