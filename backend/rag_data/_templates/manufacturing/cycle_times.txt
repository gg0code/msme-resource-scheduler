STANDARD CYCLE TIMES (minutes per piece unless noted)

--- MACHINING ---
Simple turned part (30mm dia, 100mm long):     8-12 min
Complex turned part (with boring/grooving):    20-35 min
Simple milled part (2 faces):                  15-25 min
Complex milled part (5 faces, pockets):        45-90 min
Drill + tap M8 hole:                           2-3 min per hole

--- GRINDING ---
Surface grind (100x50mm face, 0.1mm stock):    5-8 min
Cylindrical grind (30mm dia, 100mm length):    8-15 min

--- WELDING ---
Simple tack weld (per joint):                  2-3 min
MIG butt weld (per 100mm, 5mm plate):          4-6 min
TIG weld (per 100mm, 2mm SS):                  8-12 min

--- ASSEMBLY ---
Bolt joint (M8, per fastener):                 1-2 min
Press fit assembly (per component):            3-5 min
Bearing installation (per bearing):            5-8 min

--- FINISHING ---
Deburring (per piece, simple):                 3-5 min
Shot blasting (per kg of parts):               2-4 min
Powder coat (per sqm of surface):              15-20 min (including cure)

--- SCHEDULING BENCHMARKS ---
- Add 20% buffer to all cycle times for new operators
- Add 10% for first-off inspection on new jobs
- Batch changeover between different part families: 30-60 min
- Tooling prep before job start: 15-30 min
